Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering flowers online at Phoolbari.

Which Nepali flowers are available at Phoolbari?

We carry Sayapatri (Marigold), Makhamali (Globe Amaranth), Laligurans (Rhododendron), Godavari (Chrysanthemum), Chameli & Beli (Jasmine), Parijat, Kamal (Lotus), Surya Mukhi (Sunflower), and Mandar, alongside roses, lilies, tulips, and orchids.

How does the Daraz-style order and tracking system work?

Add flowers to your cart or click Buy Now, select items in your cart, apply promo code PHOOL10, enter your delivery address (or auto-fill using GPS), choose eSewa/Khalti/Fonepay/COD, and track your order status live in the Orders panel.

How do I view the flower shop at my current location?

Click Pin Shop at My Current Location in the top bar or map section. Your browser will pin the Phoolbari Express Hub directly to your live coordinates with an instant Google Maps link.
